How it works

Three steps from idea to a link you can share.

  1. Pick a template

    Start from a design made for your kind of business: a duka, restaurant, salon, school, clinic, farm and more. Your business name goes in automatically.

  2. Add your details

    Type your prices, WhatsApp number, location and M-Pesa Till or Paybill. Click any part of the page to change the words, photos and colours.

  3. Publish and share

    One click puts your site online. Share the link on WhatsApp status, Instagram or a printed flyer, and customers can add it to their phone like an app.

Questions

Before you start.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. If you can type a WhatsApp message, you can build your site. You click on the words or photos you want to change and type over them.

How do customers pay me?

Customers pay you directly with M-Pesa using the Till or Paybill number on your site. The money goes straight to you. AppWebBuilder never holds your payments.

Will it work on cheap phones and slow internet?

Yes. Sites are light, load fast on mobile data, and keep working when the connection drops once a customer has visited.

Can I use my own .co.ke domain?

Custom domains are coming to the Biashara and Pro plans. Until then, every site gets its own AppWebBuilder link that you can share anywhere.

What if I want to leave?

Export your site as HTML files from the editor at any time and upload them to any web host. You can also save a backup and restore it later.
